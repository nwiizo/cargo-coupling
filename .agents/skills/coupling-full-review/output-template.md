obtained and findings supported by inspected evidence; no fixed scoring rubric,
reviewer roster, or schedule is implied.

- Confidence or unresolved context:

## Design Choices to Preserve
[Supported observations]

## Verification and Limits
[Checks run, results, analysis blind spots, and skipped checks]

## Next Actions
[Priorities grounded in impact; owners or dates only when known]
